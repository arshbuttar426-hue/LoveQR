| E02_patterns | Separability of the 16 four-triangle cell patterns | OBSERVED (SIMULATED channel) | SIMULATED / SOFTWARE | 80 | `20261108T105559Z-9db948` | Measured mean target-cell accuracy by severity: brutal=0.314, clean=1.000, extreme=1.000, harsh=1.000, mild=1.000. | E07_compression | Shared instruction dictionary (OTL1) compression effectiveness | OBSERVED (software) | SIMULATED / SOFTWARE | 30 | `20261108T074405Z-c06991` | OTL1 compresses repetitive, textual and structured payloads strongly (highest mean ratio: zeros at 58.5×), leaves incompressible random data at roughly unit ratio (mean 0.984 (range 0.972–0.992)), and always round-trips byte-exactly. | E08_rolling | Temporal blink IDs under a rolling-shutter capture | OBSERVED (SIMULATED rolling shutter) + THEORETICAL (timing) | SIMULATED / SOFTWARE | 60 | `20261108T073316Z-5c57b4` | A 26-chip word is recovered only when the rolling readout spans at least 26 display symbol periods; the lowest tested display rate with a fitting word was 1000 Hz.
